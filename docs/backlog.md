# Backlog

This is the detailed issue and historical closure ledger. [Status](status.md) is the sole current capability and
milestone table; [development](development.md) documents validation practice and [resources](resources.md) locates
tools and references. Dated measurements and closed items retain their original evidence and scope. Neither a
passing regression suite nor a newer native engagement feature closes unrelated stability or fidelity findings.

(2026-10-11: B-19, B-28, B-30, B-35, B-40, B-41, B-42, B-51 and B-56 closed; B-32 down to the UH-60; B-21, B-22 and B-29 as the designs built again show them; B-60 to B-68 added.)

Findings measured and not yet fixed, across the platform: what the stability suite, the mass accounting and the
viewer work of 2026-10-10 left open (commit a05c5ef). It continues the numbering of
[flight-autonomy.md](architecture/modules/simulation/flight-autonomy.md)'s active backlog (B-1 to B-18, all closed there) and keeps its rule: each
item says where it was measured and what closes it, and none is dropped because it lies outside the step that found
it.

Priority: **1** an aircraft is lost or a number is wrong in normal use; **2** a limit a user will meet; **3** a gap in
fidelity or tooling that nothing depends on yet.
