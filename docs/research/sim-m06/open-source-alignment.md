## 3. Parameter Provenance & Anti-Fabrication Rules

1. **No fabricated passive range**: Passive IRST, FLIR, and DAS/MAWS contacts ALWAYS report `rangeM = NaN`, `latitudeRad = NaN`, `longitudeRad = NaN`, `heightM = NaN` unless actively ranged by a fitted LRF in `TaskKind::Track` with laser SNR above threshold.
2. **Exact physical scaling**:
   - Planck blackbody in-band fraction verified against Wien displacement and Stefan-Boltzmann limits.
   - Kinetic heating $T_{\text{skin}}$ verified against recovery temperature $T_{\text{amb}}(1 + 0.85 \cdot 0.2 M^2)$.
   - Beer-Lambert transmittance verified against $\exp(-\alpha R)$ and altitude density scaling.
   - LRF return power verified against two-way $R^{-2} \tau_{\text{atm}}^2$ Lambertian link equation.
3. **Explicit `basis: assumed` hardware bounds**: Sensor NEI, aperture diameter, LRF peak power, IFOV stabilization floor, and field-of-regard angles are engineering class defaults clearly documented as `basis: assumed` and overridden whenever a payload card supplies explicit published parameters.
4. **Honest active-optical boundary**: Active laser ranging/designation is modeled strictly on cards whose real hardware includes a laser transmitter (`an-aaq-40`, `eots-86`, `h6k-eots`, `oeps-27`, `oeps-29`, `osf`, `101ks-atoll`, `klen-ps`). Passive-only cards (`an-aaq-37`, `an-aar-54`, `an-aar-56`, `pirate`, `eord-31`, `an-asq-151`, `eiss-eoir`, `syers-2c`, `c17a-mws`, `gripen-maws`, `j20a-das`) report `laser_capable = 0` and never produce laser range measurements.
