# SIM-M07 (MS-11): Public-Literature and Open-Source Alignment (Weather, SAR, SMTI, PNT, Comm/IFF & Ground-Map)

This note documents the public literature and mature open-source references evaluated and aligned for SIM-M07 (MS-11: Weather Radar, Synthetic Aperture Radar, Surface Moving-Target Indication, PNT INS/GNSS, Tactical Comm Terminals / Mode 5 IFF, and Ground-Map Radar), fulfilling `ms-work-allocation.md` Sections 1.1 and 6.2.

## 1. Open-Source Evaluation and Reference Lineage

| **Synthetic Aperture Radar** (`sar`) | **Curlander & McDonough**, *Synthetic Aperture Radar: Systems and Signal Processing* (Wiley, 1991); **Ulaby**, *Microwave Radar and Radiometric Remote Sensing*; **Sandia SAR** spotlight/strip radiometry; **FERS** | Pure closed-form radiometric/geometric SAR equations; zero external dependency needed. | Exact Curlander–McDonough integration time $T_{\text{int}} = \frac{\lambda R}{2 v \rho_a \sin\psi}$ (enforcing side-looking squint geometry) and Noise Equivalent Sigma Zero $\sigma^0_{\text{NE}} = \frac{4(4\pi)^3 R^3 k_B T_0 F L v \sin\theta_i}{P_{\text{avg}} G^2 \lambda^3 \rho_r}$ degraded by in-band RF jamming $10\log_{10}(1 + J_0 / N_0)$, completing `TaskKind::Image` with `ContactKind::Image`. |

| **PNT (INS & GNSS)** (`pnt`) | **Paul D.
