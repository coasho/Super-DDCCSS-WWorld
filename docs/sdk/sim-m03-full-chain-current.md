## Initial baseline effect interpretation

`WorldWeaponEffectsDiagnostics.cpp::captureWeaponImpactDiagnosticsV1` records actual component impacts. Panel/blast response can produce a native target damage event without a component record. AIM-120D and R-27R therefore pass the native damage-event predicate but still fail zero component work; R-27ER fails both. This is not repaired by counting a panel or event as an internal component.

AIM-120D selects `native.fuze.swept_path.v1`: its V0 law allows the minimum at a still-approaching captured endpoint. Its actual burst time equals the outer decision boundary. `Damage.cpp` explicitly identifies the legacy five-sector profile's missing forward 0-20 degree and aft 160-180 degree sectors. Constant-velocity extrapolation of the last recorded native state suggests an approximately 8.4 m forward-target geometry; this remains a hypothesis pending an exact captured-burst/intersection observation. AIM-120C changes fuze, station and source profile simultaneously, so its pass does not isolate which difference explains D.

All selected zoned-case warheads actually emit finite particles or rods through `WarheadSnapshotV1::emitScene` and `emitFragmentFieldV1`. The R-27 selections emit a connected ring. Existing topology/material/source assumptions require physical review; shared class names are not evidence of a no-op mechanism.

## Physical reference boundary

A detector-family correction must be justified independently of the failed outcome. The current geometric sphere and generic source hypotheses do not reconstruct equipment signals. NAVAIR identifies an active-radar proximity fuze for [AMRAAM](https://www.navair.navy.mil/product/AMRAAM). [Saab's Meteor PFS statement](https://www.saab.com/newsroom/press-releases/2017/saab-receives-subsystems-order-for-meteor) distinguishes detecting passage and calculating burst time. [MBDA's MICA air-to-air datasheet, p.2](https://www.mbda-systems.com/media/20541/download) specifies RF proximity/impact and focused splinters for the family. These sources identify mechanisms; they do not supply a licence to fit hidden hardware coefficients from this scene.

[MBDA's ASRAAM datasheet, p.2](https://mbdainc.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/MBDA-ASRAAM1.pdf) identifies laser proximity/impact; [Diehl's IRIS-T brochure, printed p.9](https://www.diehl.com/cms/files/EN_Defence_e_Internet_2017.pdf) identifies radar proximity. Seeker modality cannot determine fuze modality. The [Navy fundamentals, §§15.2-15.4](https://man.fas.org/dod-101/navy/docs/fun/part14.htm) separates detection, safing/arming and initiation; the [APL Talos primary paper, pp157-159](https://www.jhuapl.edu/Content/techdigest/pdf/V03-N02/03-02-Brown.pdf) explains directional pattern/burst timing. Talos numerical hardware inputs must not become modern AAM constants.

Keep actual card scene/seed/radius/time limits and source uncertainty explicit. Do not convert23 fuzes to CPA, fill angular sectors or alter material/fragment counts merely until this encounter passes. Source-backed versioned mechanisms need their own independent method and sensitivity cases, then the original full-chain replay. Current full-program regression, installed/public and fullscreen visual acceptance remain separate required work.
